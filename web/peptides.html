Per-sequence report

Vaccine peptides for the same variant line up above every supporting reconstruction. Long proteins are trimmed to the vaccine region with up to eight flanking residues; ellipses indicate omitted sequence. Green highlights mark confirmed containment. “Sequence disagreement” means a protein was produced but the full vaccine sequence is absent or incomplete. “No reconstructed sequence” means Exacto produced no target-linked protein in the selected runs.

Rows are positioned by shared sequence, without inserting alignment gaps. References without an unambiguous shared stretch appear separately. Long regions continue in additional blocks. Identical full proteins share a row; differences outside the displayed window still produce separate rows. Identical displayed regions are labeled explicitly; each row gives the number of amino acids hidden before and after the window.

RNA support counts distinct input RNA sequences whose translated protein contains the peptide, or produces that full reconstruction. Raw reads, corrected RNAs, and assembled RNAs are counted separately by sample and method; they can reuse the same reads, so the counts must not be added together. These are sequence-support counts, not total RNA coverage at the mutation. Full proteins and supporting RNA call IDs are in the analysis download.

References and evidence

References are recorded sequences explicitly linked to a vaccine. Longer vaccine peptides and mRNA minimal epitopes retain separate entries. Matching uses the full exported protein sequences and requires the target RNA allele at the peptide’s translated position, or its frameshifted tail.
