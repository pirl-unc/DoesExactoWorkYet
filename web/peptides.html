Vaccine peptides for the same variant line up above reconstructed sequences in a 45-amino-acid window centered on the aligned vaccine region. The window expands when needed to include all aligned vaccine peptides. Green highlights mark confirmed containment. “Sequence disagreement” means a protein was produced but the full vaccine sequence is absent or incomplete. “No reconstructed sequence” means Exacto produced no target-linked protein in the selected runs.

Rows are positioned by shared sequence, without inserting alignment gaps. References without an unambiguous shared stretch appear separately. Long regions continue in additional blocks. Proteins with identical sequence and coverage within the whole comparison window share one row, even if they differ outside it. Missing ends and stop markers remain distinct. Full-protein counts and lengths preserve provenance; ellipses mean at least one member continues beyond the displayed region. Screen size changes line wrapping, not sequence grouping.

Underlined residues differ from the first window in each aligned group. Changes use 1-based window positions, not full-protein positions. “Contains P1, P2” identifies the specific recorded peptides recovered.

Window RNA support combines distinct input RNAs producing the same window sequence, counted once per sample and method. The per-peptide support below retains the full vaccine-containment checks. Raw reads, corrected RNAs, and assembled RNAs are counted separately by sample and method; they can reuse the same reads, so the counts must not be added together. These are sequence-support counts, not total RNA coverage at the mutation. Full proteins and supporting RNA call IDs are in the analysis download.
